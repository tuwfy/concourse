Read it like a revenue manager.

An index of 100 means you're at parity with your comp set. Above 100 you're capturing more than your fair share; below 100 you're leaving it on the table. RGI is the one that pays the bills — it folds occupancy and rate into a single fair-share number.

How to read it

Three indices, one comp set, zero spreadsheets. Everything indexes to 100.

The three numbers that decide next week's rate.

Index 01

MPI · occupancy

Your occupancy divided by the comp set's, times 100. Tells you whether you're filling rooms faster or slower than your competitors.

Fair share of demand
Index 02

ARI · rate

Your ADR against the set's ADR. A high ARI with a low MPI usually means you're priced ahead of the market and giving up nights.

Pricing power
Index 03

RGI · RevPAR

RevPAR vs. the set — the bottom line. It blends occupancy and rate into one fair-share figure. This is the number owners track.
