FAQ

Questions hotels usually ask before signing.

How is this different from Cvent or Hotel Planner?

We don't sell impressions. The platforms you're paying today were architected around 2008 lead generation; we built ours around 2026 data routing. Hotels on Concourse get buyer-intent signal and benchmarking back, not just listings forward.

Do planners really pay nothing?

Correct. The platform is free for planners, agencies, and corporate event teams — forever, on principle. Hotels and venues pay for placement and data access. Every economic relationship on the platform sits on the buy side.

What does setup actually look like?

One discovery call, one integration session (PMS + CRM + DMS) in a single morning, and a Friday QA. By the next Monday your team is logging into a populated workspace with your real data.

Can we trial Insights before committing?

Yes. We offer a 30-day Insights trial on the full workspace, populated with your data. If you don't see at least one actionable signal in the first two weeks, we'll wind it down with no obligation.

How are seminars priced and who pays?

Public seminars are ticketed individually (typically $295 – $1,495 depending on format). Network customers get two seats per year included. We also run private in-house sessions for brand and management groups.
