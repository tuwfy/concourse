How the benchmark math works.

Concourse follows the same definitions a STR/CoStar STAR report uses, so the numbers map cleanly onto the reports your revenue team already reads. Here is exactly how every metric on the benchmark is calculated, and where the Orlando demo data comes from.

The core three

Occupancy, ADR, RevPAR.

The three numbers everything else is built on.

Occupancy
Rooms sold ÷ rooms available
ADR · average daily rate
Room revenue ÷ rooms sold
RevPAR · revenue per available room
Room revenue ÷ rooms available
(= Occupancy × ADR)
The three indices

Everything indexes to 100.

An index compares your property to the aggregate of your competitive set. 100 is parity; above 100 you are capturing more than your fair share, below 100 you are leaving it on the table. The comp-set aggregate is room-weighted and excludes the subject hotel itself, the same way STR builds it.

Index MPI

Market Penetration

Your occupancy ÷ comp-set occupancy × 100. Are you filling rooms faster than your competitors?

Occupancy fair share
Index ARI

Average Rate Index

Your ADR ÷ comp-set ADR × 100. Are you priced above or below the set?

Rate fair share
Index RGI

Revenue Generation

Your RevPAR ÷ comp-set RevPAR × 100. The bottom line, occupancy and rate combined.

Revenue fair share
Note ↗

Reading them together

High ARI with low MPI usually means you're priced ahead of the market and giving up nights. RGI is the referee.

The full picture

About the Orlando demo data

The 30 Orlando hotels on the benchmark are real properties, names, brands, submarkets and room counts are accurate and public. Their performance figures (occupancy, ADR, RevPAR) are representative estimates modeled on public Orlando market patterns and seasonality, not licensed STR/CoStar STAR data. The index methodology, comp-set logic and seasonality are production-grade, so the workspace behaves exactly as it would on your live PMS and STR feeds. When you connect your accounts, modeled data is replaced with your actuals.
